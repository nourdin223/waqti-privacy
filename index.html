Waqti – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

1. Introduction

Waqti ("we", "our", or "the app") is a digital wellbeing application designed to help users manage their screen time and reduce excessive use of selected applications. Your privacy is important to us.

2. Information Collection

Waqti does not collect, transmit, or sell personal information to external servers. The app does not use advertising services, analytics platforms, or third-party tracking tools.

3. Accessibility Service

Waqti uses Android's Accessibility Service to detect which application is currently in the foreground. This functionality is necessary to provide application blocking and screen-time management features.

The Accessibility Service is not used to read screen content, passwords, personal messages, or other user input. Information detected by this service is processed locally on your device.

4. Local Data Storage

The app may store selected applications, blocking preferences, and other settings locally on your device to provide its functionalities.

This information is not transmitted to external servers.

5. Third-Party Services

Waqti does not integrate advertising networks, analytics services, or external data collection services.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information stored locally by the application. The app uses available Android security mechanisms for local storage.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Application settings remain on your device until you change them, clear the application data, or uninstall the app. You can remove locally stored application data through your Android device settings.

8. Children's Privacy

Waqti does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Any changes will be published on this page.
